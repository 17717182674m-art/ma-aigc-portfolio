01 Overview 项目概述

Restraint as a visual strategy.

A fragrance brand needs to look like it smells. The identity keeps a near-monochrome palette, one serif for the wordmark and an enormous amount of air — so the product and the light do the talking. The campaign extends the same restraint into motion.

画面几乎只用单一色系，靠光线、材质与留白制造层次。中文名「留白」既是品牌名，也是视觉方法：先决定留多少空，再决定放什么。
