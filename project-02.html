01 Overview 项目概述

One mark, one grid, eleven surfaces.

Yuefeng needed more than a logo. The goal was a system that could stretch from a 3:1 outdoor banner down to an app avatar without losing character. Everything is built on a strict horizontal grid with a heavy weight contrast between the mark and the type.

项目从「一个标记 + 一套网格」出发：先确定横向基准线与字号层级，再把它复制到户外大牌、电商 Banner、字体规范与 IP 形象上。核心原则是——不改变结构，只改变尺度。
